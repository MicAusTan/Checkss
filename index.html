Imperial Chess rules

Goal

  • Destroy the enemy army. The Imperials (Emperor Ⓔ, Empress Ⓧ, Heir Ⓗ) are special: with 2 or more alive there is no check. When exactly one is left it is the check target (normal check/checkmate).
  • You lose immediately if only Imperials remain (no other pieces). Stalemate is a draw. Losing the King or Queen is never a loss.

Board

  • 17×16. The corner squares of each army's 3 ranks are cut away: nothing lands there or slides through. Pawns promote on the first full-width rank in front of the enemy (and deeper) — promotion is compulsory.

Imperials

  • Immune to pawn-family captures (Pawn, Pikeman, Skirmisher). Emperor also immune to Rooks, Empress to Bishops, Heir to Knights (Grand pieces ignore this).
  • Emperor: rook lines plus the squares beside the line. Empress: diagonals plus the 4 orthogonal neighbours of each line square. A piece on the line cuts off the side squares at and beyond it. Lose your Empress → the Emperor reaches only 3 squares and loses Rook immunity (and vice-versa). Heir: 16 knight + camel leaps; when both Emperor and Empress are gone he also gets (±2,±4),(±4,±2) and loses Knight immunity.
  • Castle: Emperor hops a friendly Rook/Grand Rook, Empress a friendly Bishop/Grand Bishop, landing on an empty square beyond it (max 6 away, cooldown 3 of your turns, not while in check). Swap: the Heir may swap with a friendly Knight/Grand Knight in his reach.

King & Queen auras (5×5)

  • King: friendly pawn-family may repeat their 2-step; Rooks, Bishops, Knights and Grands can't be captured by pawn-family pieces.
  • Queen: ordinary Pawns also capture straight ahead; Archers, Guards, Chariots, Sappers, Catapults can't be captured by pawn-family pieces.

Royals

  • Prince / Princess (promotion only, one each): Prince captures on the 5×5 (no corners) and also steps to corners and 3 straight; blockable. Princess moves 1, shoots the outer ring and 3 straight without moving; she can't be captured while the Prince is next to her.

Pieces

  • Grand Rook ♖ rook + knight moves (move only). Grand Bishop ♗ bishop + 1–2 straight. Grand Knight ♘ the whole outer ring of the 5×5. Only original Rooks/Bishops/Knights become Grands (automatically on reaching the promotion rank).
  • Guard Ⓖ king moves, plus steps 2 straight or onto squares around a nearby friend; Archers can't hit it or its neighbours. Chariot Ⓒ slides 2 straight with knight squares off each lane. Archer Ⓐ steps 1; shoots 16 squares around it without moving. Sapper Ⓢ leaps, never captures; after moving it may place a wall next to itself (once per 2 turns). Catapult Ⓣ walks 1–2 straight; fires on 7 squares ahead (hitting (0,4) also splashes (±1,4)), then reloads a turn.
  • Pawn ♟ 1 step (up to 3 on its first move), captures diagonally. Pikeman ♙ also captures straight. Skirmisher ⓜ also steps sideways. Pikeman → Lancer Ⓛ, Skirmisher → Raider Ⓓ on promotion.

Piece guide

4-player free-for-all (29×29)

  • Cross-shaped board: a 21×21 centre with a 13×4 arm for each army (same 39-piece layout, rotated). Seats take turns in order; last seat standing wins.
  • Promotion happens on the far edge of the centre square (22 steps from your army). Tick Teams for 2v2 (partners sit opposite and never capture or check each other); choose the Wide board for the 30×16 version where each team's two armies sit side by side.
  • Check applies to a seat only when it has exactly one Imperial left, and any opponent can give it. A checkmated seat — or one left with only Imperials — is out; a stalemated seat just skips. Their pieces turn gray: they stop moving but can be captured, Imperials included, with no immunities.

Walls & special rules

  • Walls block movement and lines; capture one by moving onto it. Ranged shots pass over them. The number on a wall is how many full rounds it still stands (it vanishes at the start of the enemy's 4th turn in 2-player; lifetime scales with the number of seats).
  • En passant: after a 2–3 step pawn move, an enemy pawn-family piece may capture diagonally onto a square it passed, next turn only.
  • Ranged shots (⌖) capture without moving. Click a piece to see its squares.
